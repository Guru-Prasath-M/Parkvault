ParkVault Refund Policy

Refund Policy (B2B Framework)

This policy defines how cancellations and monetary refunds are synchronized between ParkVault, the Property Partner, and the End User to safeguard the Partner’s real-time slot occupancy revenue.

1. User Cancellation Matrix

  • Drivers can cancel their parking reservation up to 1 hour before the scheduled booking start time for a full refund.
  • In such cases, the reserved slot instantly updates as "Available" on the live slot radar system.
  • No payout is generated for the Property Partner when a booking is cancelled within the free cancellation window.
  • If a driver cancels less than 1 hour before the reservation or fails to occupy the reserved spot ("No-Show"), the full booking amount is charged.
  • The Partner’s revenue share will be processed during the standard Net 15 payout cycle, after deducting the platform commission fee.

2. Operational Discrepancies & Reverse Chargebacks

  • If a hardware or software issue causes an inaccurate parking overstay calculation, the driver may file a dispute within 48 hours.
  • Once verified through system logs, ParkVault may issue a dynamic refund to the driver.
  • The refunded amount will be adjusted from the Partner’s active Net 15 settlement cycle.
  • If a physical facility issue, property closure, blackout, or bottleneck prevents a pre-booked user from parking, the user will receive a full refund.
  • In such cases, the platform commission fee will be debited from the Partner’s account balance.
