ParkVault Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy (For Parking Lot Owners)

ParkVault is committed to protecting partner, supervisor, and driver information using secure infrastructure and controlled access systems.

1. Data Collected from Partners

  • ParkVault collects official business registration details, GSTIN information, and bank account credentials for payout routing and financial settlements.
  • Facility GIS location coordinates, digital floor plans, and slot naming architecture are collected during onboarding.
  • Individual supervisor details, including names and mobile numbers, are registered to access ParkVault Pulse.

2. Driver-Partner Data Segregation (The Wall)

  • Partners and supervisors are granted access only to information required for validating vehicle arrivals.
  • Supervisors can view booking status, vehicle registration numbers, active slot coordinates, and QR token verification data.
  • Full user names, phone numbers, and payment details are encrypted using SHA-256 protocols on Firebase.
  • Partners are strictly prohibited from collecting or misusing driver information outside the operational check-in process.

3. System Data Logging & Analytics Transparency

  • Data captured through supervisor interactions is securely mirrored across Firestore and BigQuery for operational analytics.
  • ParkVault uses automated analytical insights to optimize peak-hour allocations, monitor revenue performance, and eliminate parking leakages.
  • Corporate transaction logs, audit trails, and entry denial records are permanently retained in immutable ledgers.
  • These records help resolve financial audits, operational disputes, and compliance investigations seamlessly.
