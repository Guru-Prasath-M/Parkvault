1. Scope of Service & Platform Model

  • ParkVault operates as a Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) and smart parking aggregator.

  • ParkVault facilitates connections between drivers and parking property owners.
  • ParkVault does not own or operate physical parking facilities except for digital aggregation services.

3. Inventory Management & ParkVault Pulse

  • Partners agree to synchronize parking inventory in real time through the ParkVault system.
  • Supervisors must use ParkVault Pulse QR scanning during check-in and check-out.
